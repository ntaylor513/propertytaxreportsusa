Property Tax Appeal Services Agreement

Contingency fee engagement · Residential & Commercial

In short: $0 upfront. You pay 30% of your first-year tax savings only after your reduced value is final. No reduction, no fee.

1. Parties and Property

This Agreement is between Property Tax Reports USA ("Company"), Atlanta, Georgia, and the client named in the signature form ("Client"). If Client is a business entity, the person signing confirms they have authority to bind the entity that owns the Property. Additional properties may be added on a signed schedule and are billed separately under these terms.

2. Services and Authorization

Company will seek to lower the assessed value of the Property for the tax year(s) listed. Services include:

  1. Reviewing the assessment, property records, comparable sales, and income and expense data where relevant.
  2. Preparing and filing the appeal, petition, or protest by the applicable deadline.
  3. Preparing valuation evidence, including an appraisal or valuation analysis by a licensed appraiser where Company deems it necessary.
  4. Representing Client in informal reviews, settlement talks, and hearings before the county board of equalization, value adjustment board, appraisal review board, or hearing officer, as applicable.
  5. Reporting the result to Client in writing.

Client appoints Company as its authorized agent for these purposes and will sign any state agent-authorization form needed (for example, Texas Form 50-162 or a Georgia letter of authorization). Client authorizes Company to receive assessment notices and decisions on Client's behalf. Company decides the strategy, evidence, and whether to accept a settlement offer, and will inform Client before accepting a settlement. Appeals beyond the first-level board (superior court, district court, or binding arbitration) are not included unless agreed in a separate signed writing. Company does not guarantee any result.

3. Fee

No upfront fee. Client pays no retainer, filing fee, or appraisal fee to start. Contingency fee: 30% of First-Year Tax Savings, for residential and commercial properties. No reduction, no fee.

First-Year Tax Savings means the decrease in the Property's ad valorem tax for the first tax year covered by this Agreement resulting from the reduction in value: taxable value before appeal, minus taxable value after Final Determination, multiplied by the total tax rate (millage) for that year. If that year's rate has not been set when Company invoices, the prior year's rate is used. Exemptions (such as homestead) are applied the same way to both values, so Client is never charged on savings from an exemption alone. The fee is based only on the first tax year, even if the reduced value carries into later years.

Example: taxable value $500,000 reduced to $440,000; tax rate 2.5%; First-Year Tax Savings $1,500; Company fee (30%) $450; Client keeps $1,050 in year one.

Multi-year benefit. In Georgia, a value reduced on appeal is generally frozen for that year and the next two years, subject to the exceptions in Georgia law; Client keeps 100% of savings in those later years. Florida and Texas reassess every year, so later years are not covered unless Client renews.

4. When Savings Are Realized and Payment

Savings are realized at Final Determination, the first of: (a) a written settlement or agreed value accepted for Client; (b) a written notice of reduced value from the assessor or appraisal district; or (c) a written decision of the board, hearing officer, or review board reducing the value. Payment does not wait for Client to receive or pay the tax bill.

Invoice. Company will send a written invoice showing the before-and-after values, the tax rate used, and the fee, with a copy of the Final Determination. Due date: within 30 days of the invoice date.

Payment on file. Client provides a credit card, debit card, or bank account (ACH) through Company's secure payment link. No charge is made at signing. Client authorizes Company to charge the invoiced fee on or after the 15th day after the invoice date, unless Client has already paid or has disputed the invoice in writing under Section 5. Client will keep a valid payment method on file until all fees are paid. Accepted methods: card, ACH, check, or Zelle/wire. Card payments may carry a processing fee where allowed by law.

5. Client Duties, Disputes, Adjustments, and Termination

Client will: give accurate information and documents on request, including rent rolls and income and expense statements for commercial property; forward any assessment notice, tax bill, or letter about the Property within 5 business days; and not file a separate appeal, hire another agent, or settle directly with the taxing authority for the covered tax year(s) without Company's written consent.

Invoice disputes. Client may dispute the calculation in writing within 15 days of the invoice date. A dispute pauses the automatic charge for the disputed amount only.

Later changes in value. If the reduced value is later raised because of Company's error, Company refunds the matching part of its fee. No refund is due if the value is raised because Client gave inaccurate information or changed the Property. If the value is reduced further on a later appeal handled by Company, the fee is recalculated on the final reduction.

Termination. Either party may end this Agreement in writing at any time; however: if Client ends it after the appeal is filed and the value is later reduced for that year, the full fee is still owed; if Client ends it, files on its own, or hires another agent after Company has started work, Client owes the greater of the full fee on any reduction obtained for that year or Company's reasonable time and appraisal costs; and if Client prevents Company from completing the appeal, Client owes a flat fee for work performed as stated in Company's fee schedule. This Agreement covers only the tax year(s) listed unless both parties sign a renewal.

6. Late Payment, Collection, and General Terms

Late payment. Amounts unpaid 30 days after the invoice date accrue interest at 1.5% per month (18% per year), or the highest rate allowed by law if lower. Collection costs. Client pays reasonable collection costs, including agency fees, court costs, and reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent allowed by law. Future representation. Company may decline future work while any fee is unpaid. Personal guaranty (commercial only). If Client is a business entity and a Guarantor signs, the Guarantor personally guarantees payment.

Limitation of liability. Company's total liability is limited to fees Client has paid for the Property. Company is not responsible for missed deadlines caused by Client's late delivery of notices or information. Confidentiality. Company keeps Client's financial information confidential, except as needed to present evidence in the appeal. Licensing. Appraisal work is performed by a licensed appraiser; in Texas, Company or its agent is registered as a property tax consultant with the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation.

Governing law. Georgia law governs, except where the law of the state where the Property is located requires otherwise. Disputes will be heard in the courts of Fulton County, Georgia. Entire agreement. This is the full agreement; changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. If any part is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. Electronic signatures are valid.

Pricing Questions

When do I pay?

Only after the county makes your lower value official. We'll send an invoice showing your old value, new value, and savings. Payment is due within 30 days.

Why do you ask for a card?

It lets you start with no upfront payment and makes paying easy later. Nothing is charged when you sign up. You'll get an invoice first and a receipt for any payment.

What if my value isn't lowered?

You pay nothing. No filing fees, no appraisal fees, no charge for our time.

How are my savings calculated?

We take the drop in your property's taxable value and multiply it by your local tax rate for that year. Your fee is 30% of that first-year amount.

Is the fee different for commercial properties?

No. Homes, apartments, offices, retail, hotels, self storage, and other commercial properties all use the same 30% fee.

I already received an invoice. How do I pay it?

Use the secure link in your invoice text or email, or pay on our website.
